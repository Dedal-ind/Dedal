DEDAL.IN Ticketing & Refund Framework

Cancellation, Refund & Payment Policies

Governing online payments, convenience fees, ticket cancellation requests, payment failure auto-reversals, venue admissions, and refund processing mechanics across DEDAL.IN.

Operational Scope & Platform Role

DEDAL.IN operates strictly as an online technology intermediary, marketplace, and facilitation platform connecting registered users ("Users" or "Ticket Holders") with third-party event organizers, promoters, venues, and managers ("Event Organisers").

DEDAL.IN does not own, organize, host, conduct, manage, control, or determine the pricing or execution of any Event. The Event Organiser retains sole legal and operational responsibility for the Event.

15. Convenience Fees, Platform Charges & Non-Refundability

15.1. Service Consideration: DEDAL.IN levies a non-refundable Convenience Fee, Platform Service Fee, and/or Payment Processing Fee on each booking transaction.

15.2. Strict Non-Refundability: Under all circumstances—including Event cancellation, postponement, rescheduling, venue change, artist non-appearance, force majeure, or user error—all Convenience Fees, Platform Service Fees, booking surcharges, and taxes charged directly by DEDAL.IN are STRICTLY NON-REFUNDABLE.

16. Ticket Cancellation, Organiser Refund Policy & Processing Mechanics

16.1. Organiser-Governed Cancellations: All ticket cancellation requests, eligibility, refund percentages, and timelines are governed strictly by the specific Cancellation & Refund Policy established by the respective Event Organiser.

16.2. Pre-Purchase Advisory: Users are explicitly required to review Event-specific terms and refund policies prior to booking. Once a booking is completed, cancellations or refunds shall strictly be permitted only where explicitly mandated by the Event Organiser's written policy.

16.3. Refund Facilitation & Payout Sequence: Where an Event Organiser approves a refund, DEDAL.IN will process refunds back to the original payment instrument ONLY AFTER the Event Organiser has fully remitted and released the total required refund capital to DEDAL.IN. DEDAL.IN bears no financial liability to advance refunds out of its own operational funds.

16.4. Processing Timelines: Approved refunds will generally be credited to the original payment method within 7 to 14 business days, subject to banking cycles and gateway turnarounds.

17. Payment Failures, Pending Deductions & Auto-Reversals

17.1. Failed Booking Scenarios: If a User's account is debited but the transaction fails or fails to issue an official ticket due to network latency or gateway drops, a ticket has NOT been purchased.

17.2. Automated Banking Reversals: In all failed transactions, the debited amount is held by the banking gateway and will be automatically reversed back to the source account without platform deduction within 3 to 7 working days (T+2 to T+7 rules).

18. Event Cancellation, Postponement, Rescheduling & Venue Relocation

18.1. Organiser Authority: If an Event is cancelled, postponed, suspended, rescheduled, or relocated, the decision to issue a refund and the monetary quantum rests exclusively with the Event Organiser.

18.2. Absence of Platform Obligation: DEDAL.IN holds no authority, obligation, or legal duty to decide whether a refund is payable or to dictate the quantum of refund.

18.3. Communication Efforts: Upon receiving written confirmation from the Event Organiser, DEDAL.IN will make reasonable efforts to inform affected Users via SMS, email, or push notifications.

19. Exclusion of Personal Expenses, Travel & Incidental Costs

19.1. Personal Outlays: Attendance at an Event may require independent financial outlays including transportation, travel, lodging, hotel accommodations, dining, parking, or attire.

19.2. Non-Liability for Incidentals: LIMITATION OF LIABILITY: Under no circumstances shall DEDAL.IN be liable or legally required to reimburse or compensate any User for personal travel, lodging, or incidental expenses incurred as a result of Event cancellation, postponement, or venue relocation.

20. Prohibition of Chargebacks & Dispute Protocol

20.1. No Unilateral Bank Chargebacks: Users explicitly agree not to initiate payment chargebacks, payment disputes, or fraudulent debit claims with card issuers or banks for issues arising from Event cancellations or Organiser refund disputes.

20.2. Dispute Resolution Path: All refund claims must be pursued through DEDAL.IN's support framework or directly against the Event Organiser. DEDAL.IN reserves the right to recover chargeback fees, administrative charges, legal costs, and permanently suspend accounts in the event of improper chargebacks.

21. Venue Rules, Health Protocols, Security & Right of Admission

21.1. Adherence to Venue Terms: All tickets are issued subject to the specific venue regulations, security requirements, age limits, dress codes, and entry policies enforced by the Event Organiser and venue owner.

21.2. Enforcement Authority: The Event Organiser and venue management retain absolute authority to refuse entry or evict any individual who fails security screenings or violates rules.

21.3. Platform Disclaimer: DEDAL.IN assumes zero liability for admission refusals, security confiscations, or evictions.

22. Ticket Security, Resale, Scalping & Anti-Fraud Restrictions

22.1. Credentials Security: Users must keep ticket confirmation emails, SMS tokens, PDFs, dynamic QR codes, and physical passes secure. DEDAL.IN is not liable for duplicate scans, lost, stolen, or shared QR codes.

22.2. Prohibition of Resale / Scalping: Tickets purchased on DEDAL.IN are personal, non-transferable licenses. The unauthorized re-selling, scalping, commercial auctioning, or duplication of tickets for profit is strictly prohibited.

22.3. Invalidation of Scalped Tickets: Any ticket identified as being resold via unauthorized secondary platforms or scalping black markets will be invalidated immediately without refund, and entry will be barred.

23. Purchase Limits & Order Invalidation

23.1. Purchase Caps: To prevent scalping and automated bot purchasing, Events may carry ticket volume limits per user account, household, card, or phone number.

23.2. Cancellation Right: DEDAL.IN and the Event Organiser reserve the right to unilaterally cancel any order that exceeds stated limits, utilizes fraudulent payment details, or uses automated scripts/bots.

24. Force Majeure & Limitation of Platform Role

24.1. Unforeseen Events: DEDAL.IN shall not be held liable for any failure, delay, or performance interruption resulting from events beyond its reasonable control, including natural disasters, epidemics, civil unrest, or payment gateway outages.

24.2. Warranty Disclaimer: DISCLAIMER OF WARRANTIES: DEDAL.IN acts solely as a technological ticketing platform. DEDAL.IN makes no warranties regarding the quality, safety, artist lineup, or execution of any Event. Any legal disputes regarding Event execution must be directed against the respective Event Organiser.
