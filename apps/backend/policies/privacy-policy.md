# Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

At Dedalfest we strive to create quality software that you enjoy using for your business or professional life. You have a number of choices and we appreciate you giving us your business. We have created this policy that details what we will do should we fail to meet your expectations.

## Monthly Subscriptions

If at any time during your first month using our service you are dissatisfied, please contact us at dedalhelp@gmail.com. We will do our best to address your issue, provide a work around or give a timeline for a solution. If you are not satisfied, we will gladly offer you a full refund for your purchase, and downgrade your account to the free plan for that service.

## Annual Subscriptions

Dedalfest doesn't force you into an annual subscription as a condition to use our services. If at any time during your first 45 days using our service you are dissatisfied, please contact us at dedalhelp@gmail.com. If you are not satisfied, we will gladly offer you a full refund for your purchase.

## Ongoing Satisfaction

We want you to be happy with our service throughout your entire contract. If at any time during your contract we remove, break or discontinue functionality that was available at the time you signed up for our services, we ask you to notify us immediately at dedalfeedback@gmail.com. If we fail to address the breakage within any promised timelines, we will offer you a pro-rated refund for the remainder of your contract.

## Partial Downgrade of Annual Subscription

There may be situations where you wish to partially downgrade your annual subscription. Partial downgrade occurs if, during your annual subscription period, you reduce the number of user licenses, downgrade the edition of our services, or remove an add-on that you had previously purchased. In case of partial downgrade, you will be provided credits in the form of extension of subscription for the active licenses.

## Auto-Renewal

For your convenience, your monthly and yearly subscriptions will auto-renew until you cancel the service. Every time before your subscription auto-renews, we will send a mail to your registered email specifying the amount that will be charged. Similarly, after each renewal we will send you a receipt via email specifying the amount that has been deducted together with the next renewal date and amount.

## Cancellation Policy

You can cancel your subscription even up to five business days after your renewal date (for monthly subscriptions) or fifteen days after your renewal date (for annual subscriptions), and be eligible for a full refund. For cancellation requests, please email dedalpayment@gmail.com. We will not be able to entertain requests for refund after the said periods.

## Pro-Rated Refund

If at any time during your contract we remove, break or discontinue functionality that was available at the time you signed up, please notify us immediately at dedalfeedback@gmail.com. If we fail to address the issue within promised timelines, we will offer you a pro-rated refund for the remainder of your contract.

## Exception to our Refund Policy

There will be strictly no refund in cases where your account has been suspended or terminated due to violation of our Terms of Service. Refunds are also not applicable for any add-on services or third party integrations unless explicitly stated otherwise.

## Data Privacy

Dedalfest is committed to protecting your personal data. We collect only the information necessary to provide our services, including your name, email address, college information, and contact details. We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties without your explicit consent, except as required by law. For privacy concerns, please contact us at dedalsuppot@gmail.com.
